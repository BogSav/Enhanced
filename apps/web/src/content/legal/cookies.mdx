# Cookies and local storage

Last updated: November 2, 2025

This website is a static site built with React and Vite and served as static assets. We do not use cookies for analytics, advertising, or user tracking, and most pages load without setting any cookies. We only remember your UI preferences using your browser's local storage. Local storage is not a cookie, is not sent to any server, and does not identify you.

## Required

We do not set any required cookies to operate basic site features. The only persistence we use is a small local storage entry to remember your theme preference so the site opens in the same appearance you selected last time.

- Key: `enhanced.theme`
- Values: `"light"` or `"dark"`
- Scope: stored only in your browser; never transmitted
- Purpose: remember visual theme

## Hosting and operational logs

Our hosting/CDN may keep short-lived server logs (for example, IP address, user agent, requested URL) to operate the service and protect against abuse. These logs do not place cookies in your browser and are handled according to the hosting provider's policies.

## What we don't use

- No analytics cookies
- No advertising or tracking cookies
- No third-party social or profiling pixels

## Your choices

- Use the theme toggle in the site header to switch between light and dark.
- Clear the saved theme at any time by clearing site data (local storage) in your browser, or use private browsing.
- You can also block cookies in your browser settings. This site does not rely on cookies to function.

For more details about how we handle data, see our [Privacy Statement](/privacy).

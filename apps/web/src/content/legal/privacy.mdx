# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2025

This site ("Enhanced") does not collect personal data. We do not request, store, or process data from visitors through forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or similar technologies.

## What we collect

We do not collect any personal information from you. There are no contact forms, no account creation, no analytics beacons, and no advertising pixels.

## Technical logs

Our hosting/CDN provider may keep short-lived technical logs (for example, IP address, user agent, requested URL) for the purposes of operating the service, ensuring security, and diagnosing issues. These logs are handled under the provider's policies and are not used for profiling.

## Your controls

You can browse this site without providing any personal information. If you wish, you may also block cookies in your browser. The site does not rely on cookies to function.

## Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in how the site operates. The current version applies from the date shown above.
